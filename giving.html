Giving

Worshiping God with what He has given us.

Why we give

We give because God first gave to us. The Father did not spare His own Son, and in Christ He has given us every good thing.

Our tithes and offerings are an act of worship and thanksgiving. They support the preaching of the Word, the care of our church family, and the work of the gospel in Manila and to the nations.

Please give freely and as the Lord has provided. If you are visiting with us, there is no expectation for you to give.
