Ministries

Ways to learn, grow, and serve together.

01

Foundations Class

Sundays · 9:30 AM

A Sunday-morning class for anyone who wants firm footing in the Christian faith: new believers, curious visitors, and members who want to go deeper. We open the Bible together, ask honest questions, and learn the truths that hold the church together.

Looking back at how God has kept and taught His people through the centuries helps us stand more steadily today.

Now studyingChurch History

02

Men’s Fellowship

1st and 3rd Wednesdays · Online via Zoom

Men meet online to read Scripture, pray, and sharpen one another. The heart of the group is simple: love God’s Word, and let it shape how we lead our homes, handle our work, and serve the church.

It is a place to ask hard questions, be corrected gently, and grow in conviction and humility alongside other brothers.

Now studyingSex and the Supremacy of Christ, by John Piper

03

Women’s Fellowship

2nd and 4th Wednesdays · Online via Zoom

A welcoming space for women to know God more deeply through His Word and prayer. We bring real struggles and questions to Scripture, trusting the Holy Spirit to teach, comfort, and steady us.

We also pray that each woman would discover the gifts God has given her and use them for the good of the church.

Now studyingA Bible Study on Titus for Women, by Keri Folmar

04

Kid’s Sunday School

Sundays · 9:30 AM

Bible lessons for the children of our church, made to fit their age with stories, activities, and take-home work. Through Scripture we point them to Jesus and the beauty of the gospel.

While the kids learn, parents can enjoy fellowship and then talk about the lesson together at home. It is one way we live out our covenant promise to bring up our children in the training and instruction of the Lord.

Now teachingNew City Catechism

05

Pasay Kids Ministry

A Bible study for the children of Barangay 33 in Pasay. Each time we gather, the kids hear the good news of Jesus and see His love shown through people who care about them.

It is our way of loving the neighbors closest to us, and of opening a door for whole families to hear the gospel.
